A small software update

Apparently, I needed one.

So… I did some thinking. Like, actual thinking.

Not my usual “stare at the ceiling for seven minutes and somehow end up thinking about food” kind of thinking.

I sat with everything you told me. And, probably for the first time in a long time, I also sat down and tried to understand myself.

Turns out there were a lot of tabs open in my brain that I didn’t even know were running.

I’ve started understanding some things I really wish I’d understood sooner.

What I hear now

You were trying to tell me something.

You wanted more than just time together. You wanted to feel me there.

More calls. More little messages. More curiosity about your day. More conversations that went somewhere deeper than what we were eating or doing tomorrow.

You wanted me to take initiative sometimes instead of making you feel like the unpaid CEO of our relationship. And you wanted to feel cared for without always having to explain how.

“I want to feel more connected to you.”

I understand now that it wasn’t really about how many hours we spent together. It was about how we spent them and how connected we felt while we were together.

Meanwhile, behind the scenes

Inside my ridiculously complicated brain…

Something else was happening too. The strange part is that I didn’t understand it while it was happening.

There were little moments when I felt disconnected, unheard, uncertain, or hurt. A call wouldn’t get answered. A conversation would suddenly stop. Sometimes I’d suggest something and feel like my preference didn’t really matter.

None of those moments seemed big enough for me to say, “Emma, this is affecting me.” So I didn’t.

Brilliant communication strategy, I know. 10/10. No notes.

Instead, I made quiet interpretations: maybe she’s busy; maybe she doesn’t feel like talking; maybe this isn’t important to her. I’ll just leave it.

And without consciously deciding to, I gradually pulled back.

The part I wish I understood then

This is where I went wrong.

Feeling those things wasn’t wrong. Not communicating them was.

I wasn’t consciously thinking, “Emma did this, so now I’m going to become distant.” I wasn’t processing my emotions deeply enough to understand that I was becoming distant.

Instead of saying, “I’ve been feeling disconnected lately,” or asking, “Is that actually what you mean?” I kept everything inside and let my behavior do the communicating.

Unfortunately, my behavior apparently speaks a language neither of us understands.

I can understand how that distance could have looked like I wasn’t putting in effort or didn’t care enough. I wish I had given you the explanation that I didn’t even know how to give myself yet.

A brief legal disclaimer

What I don’t want this website to say.

I did this. You did this. The perfect blame game.

No. This isn’t my courtroom presentation.

I’m not trying to prove that you were wrong, that I was right, or to replace your feelings with my explanation.

Your experience was real even if mine was different. I’m finally beginning to understand my experience too. I think both deserve to be heard.

What mattered underneath it all

The part you were right about.

You wanted connection. And I think I confused being physically present with always being emotionally present.

We could spend an entire day together and still miss the conversations that mattered more than another five hours sitting next to each other.

How are you, actually?What’s been bothering you? What do you need from me?Is something I’m doing hurting you? Am I carrying something I haven’t told you?

I understand what you meant now.

An important limitation

I don’t suddenly understand everything.

One small problem with my brilliant analysis: there were two people in this relationship, and I’ve only had access to one brain. Mine was complicated enough.

I don’t want to tell you what you felt. I don’t want to assume why you did certain things. And I don’t want to convince myself that I’ve solved us because I’ve spent time understanding myself.

There are things only you can help me understand.

I want to know what those months felt like from your side. I don’t want to defend myself. I genuinely want to hear you.

So, Emma…

If you made it this far, congratulations.

You have survived both this website and an unusually high concentration of Pritesh thoughts. Your prize is unfortunately… more Pritesh. Just for one last minute. ❤️

What I really want is a genuine conversation with you. No scorekeeping. No pressure to have an answer about us. Just two people finally talking about the things we didn’t know how to talk about before.

If you’re comfortable having that conversation, I’d really like to have it. And this time, I promise I’ll do a lot more listening than website development. ❤️
